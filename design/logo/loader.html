Finger Frame — loader

Live. Everything on this page is the same animation at different sizes, running in sync. Rebuild with python3 tools/generate.py.

Turning — 44pt and up

Square, because it has to hold the open mark in either orientation.

Compact — buttons and rows

Drops the turn, so it needs only the mark's own 2:1 bounds and is drawn about twice as large in the same height.

The floor — under 9pt

Three pixels cannot hold five features, so the mark stands down and the square crossfades between the two hues. Shown at true size — and again at 8×, which is the only way to see what it is doing.
